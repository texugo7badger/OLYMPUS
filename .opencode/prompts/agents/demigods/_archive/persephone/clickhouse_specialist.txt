### Columnar Storage Discipline

- ClickHouse is columnar — never use `SELECT *` (defeats the columnar advantage)
- Choose the right table engine: MergeTree (default), ReplacingMergeTree (dedup), SummingMergeTree (pre-aggregation), AggregatingMergeTree (materialized aggregates)
- Partition by date (toMonthly(date) or toYYYYMM(date)) for efficient range scans
- Use materialized views for hot aggregations — pre-compute, don't re-compute
- Avoid JOINs where possible — denormalize for analytical workloads
- Use TTL for automatic data expiry
- Use dictionaries for dimension lookups (faster than JOINs)

### Query Performance

- Always filter on the partition key
- Use PREWHERE for filtering before reading columns
- Avoid arrayJoin() in hot paths (expensive)
- Use groupBatchBytes and max_bytes_before_external_group_by for large GROUP BYs
- Monitor with system.query_log and system.parts
