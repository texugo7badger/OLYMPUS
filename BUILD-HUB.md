## Gates

A build is only "verified" when at least one check is executable by `src/lib/hop-runtime/verify.ts`:
`build`, `lint`, `test`, `typecheck`, `visual[:json]`, `file-exists:<rel>`, `file-contains:<rel>::<text>`, `file-omits:<rel>::<text>`, `mounted:<artifact>::<entry>`, `design-score:<n>`.
Everything else is prompt guidance and is documented in [BUILD-VERIFY.md](BUILD-VERIFY.md). `builds:check` fails a build with guidance-only checks.
